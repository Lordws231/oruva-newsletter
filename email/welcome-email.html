Welcome to The Oruva Newsletter.

We are excited to have you join a growing community of riders, drivers, founders, operators, students and builders shaping the future of movement across Nigeria.

The Oruva Newsletter is Oruva Transportation Services' newsletter, where we share education on the transport system and its services, innovation, and the opportunities around them — capital, partnerships, grants and scholarships. Our goal is simple: signal over noise, so you gain knowledge and discover opportunities you can actually use.

Here's what you can expect from us:

  • Fresh Roads – insights and opinions from the Oruva team on transport and Nigeria's evolving markets.
  • Transport Explained – the transport system, simply taught: fares, commissions, verification, safety, regulation.
  • Innovation – mobility technology and ideas, in Nigeria and beyond.
  • Capital & Opportunities – capital opportunities, grants and scholarships, with deadlines and who can apply.
  • Partnerships – partner spotlights and open calls to work with Oruva.
  • Spotlight – stories of exceptional drivers, riders, founders and operators.
  • Expert on the Road – practical lessons from people who run transport every day.
  • Resources – one recommended read or tool in every edition.
  • Opportunities Board – jobs and openings across Oruva and our partners.
